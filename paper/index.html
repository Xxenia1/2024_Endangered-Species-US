Data & Preprocessing

The current workflow brings several state-level and spatial inputs into a common analytical frame without asserting that they represent one identical year or measurement process.

Method

Species records, population, GDP, risk, land-cover, protected-area, and state-boundary inputs are cleaned, standardized, aggregated, and joined at the state level. The current implementation uses standardized state names as the join key; the source files and year references should be finalized before publication.

Methods

Feature Engineering

Derived variables transform raw counts, areas, percentages, and source risk scores into comparable state-level indicators for exploration.

Method

Area-normalized measures use state area in an equal-area projection. Land-cover fields are class proportions. Human Pressure and the species-density component of Biodiversity Risk use Min-Max normalization across the 51-state dataset. No Z-score normalization is currently applied.

Human Pressure

Composite indicator

Biodiversity Risk Index

The current index is a transparent, rule-based composite intended to organize exploratory comparisons. It is not a probability, causal estimate, or validated predictive model.

Method

Let D be Min-Max normalized Species Density, H be Human Pressure divided by 100, R be Risk Overall divided by 100, and P be Protected Area Coverage divided by 100.

Interpretation

Discussion & Future Work

The system is a research prototype for organizing complex environmental evidence, not a completed ecological decision model.

Discussion

Coordinated views place dispersed variables into one analytical environment and support movement from national overview to state-level detail. Derived indicators help organize complex variables, while direct interaction reduces the cost of switching between spatial, ranked, and contextual representations.

The system supports exploratory analysis and cannot replace ecological protection decisions. Composite indicators depend on variable selection, normalization choices, and manually specified weights.

Future work

  • County-level or finer-scale analysis
  • Temporal comparison
  • Improved ecological validation
  • Scenario-based filtering
  • Natural-language query support
  • Usability evaluation
  • Transparent indicator weighting
